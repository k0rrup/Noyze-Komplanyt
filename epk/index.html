Bio

NOYZE KOMPLAYNT is a half-Persian club producer and DJ out of San Diego, raised between Los Angeles and San Diego on Middle Eastern music, rap, rock, metal and techno. The current run is tech house with hip-hop weight, heavy 126-BPM grooves built for rooms with real subs. He came up throwing parties before making the music for them, and now runs his own San Diego/Los Angeles club night, LOCAL FREQUENCIES.
